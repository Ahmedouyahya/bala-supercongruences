## Section 2 (Class K)

### Issue 4: Lemma 7 proof assumes Lemma 1 gives \(C(mp^t,pi'')\equiv C(mp^{t-1},i'')\pmod{p^t}\) for all \(t\)
- **Location**: Line 44, “\(C(mp^t,pi'')^2\equiv C(mp^{t-1},i'')^2\) (Lemma 1)”.
- **Claimed**: The Kazandzidis quotient (Lemma 1) yields this congruence modulo \(p^t\).
- **Why it fails**: The classical Kazandzidis congruence is \(C(p^r a, p^r b)\equiv C(p^{r-1}a,p^{r-1}b)\pmod{p^{3r}}\). Here we have \(C(p\cdot mp^{t-1}, p\cdot i'')\) — only **one** extra factor of \(p\), not \(p^t\). The standard result gives \(\pmod{p^3}\), which is insufficient when \(t\ge 3\). The proof in Class (S) applied Lemma 1 with \(s=1\) and obtained modulus \(r+1+\min(r,1)=r+2\); if that same generalized Lemma 1 is used here with \(r=t\), it would give modulus \(t+2\ge t\), which would suffice. But **the exact statement of Lemma 1 is not in this document**, so we cannot confirm it covers this case.
- **Severity**: **GAP** (depends on the missing Lemma 1 having the required strength).

### Issue 5: Reduction \(S_K\equiv \sum_k' k^{-2}\Psi_1(M,[k/p])\pmod{p^r}\) uses Lemma 4(ii) on \(C(2N-k-pi',N)\)
- **Location**: Lines 39–41.

- **Why it’s unclear**: Lemma 4(ii) is described as “\(C(j,mp^r)\equiv C(\lfloor j/p\rfloor, mp^{r-1})\pmod{p^r}\)”. Here the lower index is \(N=mp^r\), so it matches. The application is correct **if** Lemma 4(ii) is exactly as stated. Since Lemma 4 is from the missing note, this is another dependency on an unverified external lemma.
- **Severity**: **GAP** (conditional on the missing note).

The second congruence (\(r=1\)) proof is also structurally correct but relies on unproven first-order expansions and a non-standard Kazandzidis variant for \(C(pA-1, pB)\).

### Three steps I am least sure about:

Deriving them rigorously requires careful \(p\)-adic analysis of products like \(\prod_{j=1}^k \frac{mp-j}{j}\); it is not obvious that the harmonic sum \(H'_k\) appears with the claimed coefficients.

3. **Valuation bound for \(Q_4\) in Class (S)** (line 30): The claim \(v_p(2M-K'-I'), v_p(M-K'-I') \ge \min(r-1,\mu-1)\) is used to deduce the modulus for \(Q_4\). No proof is given, and it involves possible cancellation in the base-\(p\) digits of \(K',I'\) relative to \(M\). A counterexample could exist if the digits align badly, so this step must be justified.
