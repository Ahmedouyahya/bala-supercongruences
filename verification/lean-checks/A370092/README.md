# Machine check of A370092

Result: Bala's conjecture on A370092, in the form of the benchmark target `oeis_370092_conjecture_0` (for every k > 2, the sequence a(n) mod k is eventually periodic with a period dividing φ(k)), is proved in Lean and accepted by every check below. Checked on 2026-10-16. The proof takes the period P = φ(k) and the start n ≥ k + 2.

The proof is elementary and differs from `proofs/A370092.md` in one place: the paper proves Lemma B (the powers of two) with Amice's theorem on Mahler coefficients of locally analytic functions, which is not available in Lean. Here Lemma B is proved with finite sums only (plan: `scripts/PLAN.md`, Part 3): from (y−1)^{2a+e} = (y−1)^e ((y²−1) − 2(y−1))^a, the k-th finite difference of f(x) = x^n(x^{2^m} − 1) splits into differences of the polynomials t ↦ f(l + 2t) on the two residue classes mod 2, and (1+2t)^{2^m} = 1 + 2^{m+1} t(t+1) + 2^{m+2} R(t). This gives v_2(b_k) ≥ m + 1 + v_2(⌊k/2⌋!) (the bound the paper obtains from Amice's theorem) and, for ⌊k/2⌋ ≠ 2, one more factor 2; Lemma B follows for every m ≥ 1, which also covers the modulus 4 (the paper treats it by a separate computation). Numerical check of the new bounds (about 11,000 cases each, 0 failures, minimum slack 0): `outputs/elementary_lemmaB_test.out`.
