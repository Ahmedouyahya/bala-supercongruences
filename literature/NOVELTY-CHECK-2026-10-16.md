# Novelty check of 2026-10-16 (web, arXiv, OEIS JSON, Mathlib master, GitHub)

1. Theorem F ([x^{cN}]((1+x)^a(1−x)^b)^N, cubic tower for p ≥ 5).
   - b = 0: Jacobsthal–Kazandzidis (Meštrović, arXiv:1111.3057, eq. (39)).
   - c = a (and c = b via x → −x), any sign of the other exponent: A. Straub, "Multivariate Apéry numbers and supercongruences of rational functions", Algebra & Number Theory 8 (2014), arXiv:1401.0854, Thm 3.2(b) with λ = (1,1), ε = −1: A(n1,n2) = Σ(−1)^k C(n1,k)C(n2,k) = [x^{n1}](1+x)^{n1}(1−x)^{n2} (identity checked with sympy by the checker). Hence the towers of A002003 (1,−1,1), A156894 (1,−2,1), A234839 (1,2,1) are already known (A234839: "proved in 2014 by Osburn et al.", arXiv:1312.2195).
   - c ∉ {a, b} (A119259, A333561, A333562, A352373, A103885): not covered; still Bala's conjecture on the OEIS (A352373 entry: "Conjecture: for each r and s the above supercongruences hold"; Bala's A002003 PDF 2020, eq. 12).
   - Weaker/different: Beukers–Vlasenko Dwork crystals III (arXiv:2105.14841) Prop. 5.11 (p^{2s}); Mellit–Vlasenko (arXiv:1306.5811); Gorodetsky (arXiv:1805.01254). Coster's thesis, Gessel 1982, Samol–van Straten, Delaygue, Rowland–Yassawi: not read in full.
   - p = 3 criterion: nothing found; its b = 0 case is in Jacobsthal's exponent (Straub Lemma 5.1, q = v_p(p³ab(a−b)/12)).
   - Consequence for the paper: Theorem F is new for c ∉ {a,b} (with rbajaj5's earlier draft claim); credit Straub 2014 for c = a; do not call A002003/A156894 by-products.

## Second check, 2026-10-16 (evening)

1. **Beukers (1985).** The printed article (J. Number Theory 21 (1985) 141–155; DOI 10.1016/0022-314X(85)90047-2; read from the journal scan at https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~zeilberg/Combo20/projects/beukers85.pdf, page images) prints, on p. 149, the unnumbered display before (10): "it suffices to show Σ_{n=0}^{p^t−1} n^k Σ′_{λ=1}^{n} 1/λ ≡ 0 (mod p^t)", and the Abel-summation step after it uses Σ′_{λ≤n+1} − Σ′_{λ≤n} = 1/(n+1), which fails when p | n+1. Exact computation: S = Σ_{n<p^t} H′_n = p^t H′_{p^t−1} − (p^t − p^{t−1}) has v_p(S) = t − 1 exactly for p ∈ {5, 7, 11, 13}, t ∈ {1, 2, 3} (for p = 5, t = 1: S = 77/12 ≡ 1 (mod 5)). Lemma 4(ii) is printed with modulus p^{t+s}; its proof on p. 149 sums only the block i = 0. Lemma 4(i) itself holds in every case tested. The two places described in `beukers1985-shifted-method.md` and in `proofs/A376462-shifted-congruence.md` §8 are therefore confirmed against the printed paper.
